Live Performance
Touring & Broadcast

A musician built for the stage.

Grace has performed at the highest levels of Irish traditional music — from national television broadcasts on RTÉ and TG4 to touring across the UK and Ireland with Rhythm of the Dance, one of Ireland's most acclaimed stage productions.

A University of Limerick scholarship took Grace to Milwaukee Irish Festival — the largest Irish festival in North America — where she performed and taught at the summer school. She has played as a live soloist at Oireachtas 2019, broadcast on RTÉ Radio na Gaeltachta, and is a current member of the National Folk Orchestra of Ireland.

Grace is available for touring productions, festival appearances, session work and live engagements across Ireland and internationally. She plays harp, uilleann pipes, Irish flute, low whistle, tin whistle, piano, concertina and sings in English and Sean Nós.
