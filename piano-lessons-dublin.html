Your teacher

Piano lessons for
children and adults

Grace Galligan teaches classical piano from first lesson through grade exams, in person around Dún Laoghaire and South Dublin or online worldwide. She holds an MSc in Music Performance from the University of Limerick and teaches at Walton's School of Music, Dublin.

Lessons focus on solid technique, sight-reading and musicality — a calm, encouraging approach for young beginners, and a structured path toward grades or simply playing well for older students.

Who this is for
Children starting out, adults returning to piano or beginning for the first time, exam candidates.
Common questions

Piano lessons,
answered.

What age can a child start piano?

Most children begin from age 5–6, though Grace assesses readiness individually.

Do you prepare students for exams?

Yes — RIAM and ABRSM grade preparation, alongside lessons purely for enjoyment.

Can adults start from scratch?

Absolutely. Adult beginners are very welcome at any age.

Which areas do you cover in person?

Dún Laoghaire, Monkstown, Blackrock, Stillorgan and the surrounding South Dublin area — and online anywhere.
